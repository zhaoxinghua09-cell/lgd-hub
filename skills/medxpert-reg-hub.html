What it is / 是什么

All-in-one knowledge hub for medical-device registration engineers: global pathways and classification (China NMPA, US FDA, EU MDR, Japan PMDA, ASEAN and LATAM) plus 20 topic hubs - UDI, STED technical file, biocompatibility, GMP, post-market, labelling and IFU, risk, clinical evaluation, design control, software, sterilization, submission, review, after-sales, market access - mapped to the seven-stage lifecycle with verified official links. Use when you need a regulatory pathway, a classification decision or an official source fast.
