SYNOMOSAI · LGD
A governance-engineering line for AI systems · 面向 AI 系统的治理工程线

Every self-governing thing should be registered, evidenced, and gated. 凡自治之物，有籍 · 有证 · 有门禁

LGD — the Lifecycle Governance Doctrine — is a civil-society research framework for governing autonomous systems across their whole life: from registration, through evidence, to the gates that control every change, and retirement.

LGD（全程治理论）是一套面向自治系统的全程治理框架：自登记始，凭证据行，经门禁变，至退场止。这扇门通往整个生态——理论、参考实现、静默失败目录、技能库与 13 篇域论文。

LGD-I · RegistryEvery entity is registered at birth.有籍 — 出生即登记
LGD-II · EvidenceEvery key act leaves verifiable evidence.有证 — 凡行必留痕可核
LGD-III · GatesEvery evolution passes a gate.有门禁 — 凡变必过门
